My Real Estate Philosophy

You should understand the decisions you’re making.

Clear explanations. Honest answers. Time to weigh your options. I help you understand the property, the numbers, and the tradeoffs. You stay in control.

Buying or selling a house is a project. It should be managed that way.

Preparation, pricing, negotiations, inspections, and deadlines are connected. I coordinate the moving parts and keep you informed about what happens next.

Your property deserves more than a market average.

I examine relevant sales, current competition, pricing patterns, and time on market to help you decide where to price, what to offer, and when to adjust.
