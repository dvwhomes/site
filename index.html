My Real Estate Philosophy

You choose the destination. I help you get there.

Start with what matters to you. I help you understand your options, weigh the tradeoffs, and work through the obstacles. My job is to help you make your decision, not make it for you.

A transaction this important deserves a plan—and someone managing it.

We identify the risks, recognize the opportunities, and build a plan around your goals. I coordinate preparation, pricing, negotiations, and deadlines, following through on the details and adjusting when conditions change.

If you’re not looking at the right data, you’re guessing.

A metro average can’t tell you what a particular home is worth. I examine relevant sales, competing listings, pricing changes, and time on market to help you judge a price, shape an offer, or decide when to adjust.

Real estate is serious business. You should expect more from your REALTOR®.

A real estate decision involves more than price. Property condition, improvement costs, ownership responsibilities, timing, and negotiation all matter. My background helps me consider these together, not in isolation.

What I handle when you buy or sell.

When you sell

  • Walk through your home and develop a preparation plan.
  • Analyze comparable sales and competition to recommend a listing price.
  • Coordinate photography, listing materials, and showing arrangements.
  • Compare offers and negotiate price, terms, and inspection requests.
  • Track paperwork, deadlines, and closing coordination.

When you buy

  • Identify suitable properties and arrange showings.
  • Evaluate price, condition, and questions worth investigating.
  • Prepare offers and negotiate price and terms.
  • Coordinate inspections and help you evaluate findings and repair requests.
  • Track financing and contract deadlines, the final walkthrough, and closing coordination.
