About

Before residential real estate, and after a B.S. in Engineering, I spent 16 years leading multimillion-dollar facilities and construction operations at Prairie Meadows.

As Facilities Director I managed construction projects up to $36M, led 200+ employees, and ran an $11M annual operating budget.

That experience changed how I look at real estate. I don't just compare finishes or square footage—I evaluate the systems, condition, and factors that influence long-term value. Pricing follows the same approach: start with the data, understand the market, and make decisions based on evidence rather than assumptions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why work with an engineer as your REALTOR®?

I hold a B.S. in Engineering and spent 22 years in construction management, including 16 years as Facilities Director at Prairie Meadows overseeing projects up to $36M. That background shapes how I evaluate properties, assess condition, and price listings — with the same discipline used on commercial construction projects, applied to residential real estate.

What areas do you serve?

I work throughout the Des Moines metro, with a focus on Waterbury, Salisbury Oaks (SONA), Westwood, Greenwood, and nearby neighborhoods, where older housing stock benefits most from a construction and renovation background.

Do you work with buyers as well as sellers?

Yes. The same data-driven pricing and property assessment process applies whether you're buying your first home or selling one you've lived in for years.

How is your approach different from a typical agent?

My process follows the same structure I used managing construction projects: market analysis, property assessment, pricing strategy, marketing, inspection review, and transaction management — each step grounded in data rather than guesswork.

Do you help sellers prepare a home before listing?

Yes. Before a home goes on the market, I walk it with a construction-management eye — flagging issues that need attention and separating those from items that aren't worth the money, using the same assessment process described in my approach above.

What does your market analysis include?

Hyperlocal data specific to your property and neighborhood, used to build a pricing strategy based on market data rather than gut feelings — the same analytical approach used in commercial project budgeting.
